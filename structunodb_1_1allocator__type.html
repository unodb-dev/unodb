Detailed Description

Pluggable allocator for ART trees.

alloc and dealloc must be non-null. defer_dealloc must be non-null when deferred deallocation is used and may otherwise be null. ctx is forwarded to every callback and may be nullptr.

defer_dealloc schedules destroy_callback for an allocation that cannot be reclaimed immediately.

void(* unodb::allocator_type::defer_dealloc) (void *ptr, std::size_t size, destroy_callback_type destroy_callback, void *ctx)

Schedule deferred deallocation of ptr (size bytes).

Calls destroy_callback when reclamation is safe. destroy_callback is dealloc, so the allocation is reclaimed through the same allocator; it may run on another thread and after the deferring one has exited, so dealloc and ctx must outlive all pending deferrals.
