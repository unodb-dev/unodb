Detailed Description

Default leaf policy — matches the existing VIS (Value-In-Slot) behavior.

When no explicit Heap is provided to db/olc_db (Heap=void), this policy applies. The full key is encoded in the inode path, leaves can be eliminated entirely (value packed into the parent inode's child slot).
