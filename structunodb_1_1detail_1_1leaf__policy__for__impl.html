template<typename Heap, typename Value, typename = void>
struct unodb::detail::leaf_policy_for_impl< Heap, Value, typename >

Primary template: maps a Heap type to its internal leaf_policy struct.

The primary template resolves to default_leaf_policy (Heap=void). The partial specialization below selects heap_leaf_policy for any non-void Heap type.
