Detailed Description

RAII guard that clears Google Test's break_on_failure and throw_on_failure flags for its lifetime and then restores them.

A failure that a test intercepts on purpose, with EXPECT_NONFATAL_FAILURE or a testing::ScopedFakeTestPartResultReporter, still traps or throws under these flags. Declare this guard around just the intercepted statement: the flags are process-wide, so no failure reported on any thread during its lifetime traps or throws. With EXPECT_NONFATAL_FAILURE, declare it inside the statement argument rather than around the macro, whose check that the failure happened runs after the statement:

EXPECT_NONFATAL_FAILURE(
{ const unodb::test::intercepted_failure_guard guard; stmt; }, substr);
